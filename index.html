About Elarven

A considered approach to the energy transition.

Elarven Energy Solutions is a California-based company focused on renewable energy infrastructure and integrated energy solutions.

Our work centres on market insight, technical coordination, responsible planning and collaboration with qualified specialists and partners. We believe sound decisions begin with reliable information, clear responsibilities and a practical understanding of how energy systems work together.

Meet the Founder

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Dmitri Glenmoore

Dmitri Glenmoore founded Elarven Energy Solutions on a simple conviction: renewable infrastructure should be engineered as one coordinated system and delivered with the rigour investors and grid operators expect.

He holds a degree in Mechatronics Engineering, a discipline that brings together mechanical systems, power electronics, control and software. This multidisciplinary grounding informs Elarven’s approach to turbines, energy storage and plant controls—not as isolated components, but as parts of an integrated energy system designed around the needs of the wider energy system.

A pioneer in engineering, infrastructure management and development, Dmitri leads Elarven’s strategy, technical standards and partnerships. He champions evidence-based development: verified site data first, commitments second.

“Build the whole system around verified evidence. Make every commitment with a clear understanding of the engineering, risks and responsibilities.”

Principles

01 Engineer the whole system, not just the components.
02 Let measured data—not assumptions—guide design and performance decisions.
03 Plan maintenance, recycling and end of life from the first design review.
04 Be transparent with partners about risk.
